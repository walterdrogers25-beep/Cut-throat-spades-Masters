# CUT THROAT SPADES MASTERS
# VISUAL REDIRECTION & ENVIRONMENT ART BIBLE
## Authoritative visual direction for Muse — use with all five supplied reference boards

**Purpose:** Replace the generic/Victorian visual interpretation with the approved modern international luxury direction. These five images are visual references for the intended environments, not finished game assets and not permission to reproduce a single static collage as a room.

## COPY THIS FIRST — NON-NEGOTIABLE DIRECTION TO MUSE

The current visual implementation is not matching the approved vision. Stop extending the Victorian/generic interior direction. Do not use Victorian, Gothic, Baroque, palace, old-world manor, antique-library, or generic casino styling as the default.

The approved direction is **modern international luxury real estate + contemporary private members' club + photorealistic cinematic 3D game environments**.

Think world-class contemporary penthouse architecture, ultra-premium international hotels, modern private members' clubs, luxury high-rise residences, contemporary yacht-club sophistication, and cinematic architectural visualization.

The supplied five reference boards define the mood, materials, lighting, spatial quality, brand presence, and range of rooms. Use them as a consistent visual family. Do not copy their collage layout into the game. Build each location as an explorable, navigable 3D environment with actual geometry, materials, lighting, interactions, characters, and camera movement.

### Visual language
- Contemporary architecture: clean geometry, tall glass walls, expansive views, sculptural staircases, double-height spaces, carefully composed proportions, modern ceiling details, and open-plan layouts.
- Premium materials: black and warm charcoal stone, book-matched marble, brushed champagne gold, dark bronze, smoked glass, polished concrete, rich walnut, onyx, ribbed wood, leather, velvet used sparingly, and high-quality textiles.
- Palette: black, charcoal, warm ivory, deep emerald/green felt, champagne gold, subtle bronze, and controlled accents of midnight blue or violet for special events.
- Lighting: cinematic but believable architectural lighting; layered indirect cove lighting, recessed architectural fixtures, elegant pendants, statement chandeliers used selectively, illuminated display niches, warm practical lamps, city glow, realistic reflections, and controlled highlights. Avoid flat uniform lighting and excessive gold everywhere.
- Windows and views: floor-to-ceiling glazing, believable city skylines, waterfronts, mountains, desert horizons, or destination-specific landscapes.
- Furnishings: contemporary designer furniture, sculptural seating, premium leather, modern bar stools, bespoke cabinetry, modern art, integrated technology, and custom tables.
- Branding: elegant Cut Throat Spades Masters spade-and-crown identity, restrained and premium. Use consistent brand marks on key walls, table felt, room signage, trophy plinths, menus, and event screens. Avoid oversized repetitive logos on every surface.
- Rendering: photorealistic real-time 3D, physically based materials, accurate reflections, global illumination where practical, realistic shadows, cinematic camera composition, high-detail textures, and natural human scale.
- Mood: confident, exclusive, international, contemporary, welcoming, and aspirational — never dusty, old-fashioned, haunted, or museum-like.

### Absolute gameplay rule
There are **exactly four seated competitors** at a Spades table. Never create a fifth or sixth player. Butlers, bartenders, servers, cigar attendants, spa/massage attendants, hosts, and concierges are service NPCs only; they are not competitors and must not occupy player seats.

## PLAYER, STAFF, AND GAMEPLAY STAGING

- Exactly four player seats at every active Spades table.
- Four visible player avatars/characters only.
- The table must provide readable card placement, clear seat assignment, and camera access to each player.
- Human characters should be realistic, diverse, individually recognizable, and animated naturally.
- Player gestures: card selection, card placement, thinking, reacting, celebrating, and disappointment.
- Service NPCs: concierge, butler, bartender, server, cigar attendant, massage/spa attendant. NPCs move through service routes and never count as players.
- No NPC should block card visibility, obscure player turns, or interfere with the match.
- Optional drinks, cigars, spa, and butler services are cosmetic/social experiences only; they must not change card distribution, scoring, AI, or match outcomes.

## TECHNICAL ART AND IMPLEMENTATION DIRECTION

- Build actual real-time 3D spaces, not static backgrounds or image collages.
- Use the project's existing engine and architecture after auditing it. If Babylon.js is the chosen engine, use optimized scene graphs, glTF/GLB assets, PBR materials, environment lighting, shadows, and appropriate post-processing.
- Create reusable architectural modules and furniture libraries, but give each room and destination a distinct layout and visual identity.
- Use separate lighting rigs and ambient audio per destination.
- Add interaction points: doors, elevators, service counters, trophies, displays, destination selectors, replay controls, and customization stations.
- Use LOD, texture compression, instancing, baked lighting where appropriate, and on-demand room loading for mobile performance.
- Keep gameplay UI readable over the environment. Never sacrifice card readability for visual effects.
- Provide graphics quality settings and measure performance on target devices.
- Do not use unlicensed third-party art, music, logos, or branded architectural assets.
