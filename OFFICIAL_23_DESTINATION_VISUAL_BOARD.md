# OFFICIAL 23 DESTINATION VISUAL BOARD — CUT THROAT SPADES MASTERS

Status: APPROVED visual direction — November 26, 2026
Owner: Walter Rogers

## Purpose

This document and its accompanying visual reference images define the intended appearance and experience of the actual playable 3D game.

This is not a request for another concept proposal. It is the visual production specification for the existing Cut Throat Spades Masters project. The visuals in this document must be translated into actual playable 3D environments, not just promotional pictures.

Approved references: the official visual board (`masters-official-visual-board.jpg`), the 3D environment render (`lunar-outpost-3d-environment-reference.jpg`), and the table anchor photo (`mumbai-table-reference.jpg`).

## 1. The game table — four players only

Exactly FOUR players participate at the Spades table.

- Four individual seats.
- Four realistic adult human characters.
- Distinct international appearances, faces, hairstyles and wardrobes.
- Realistic card-holding animations.
- Realistic dealing, cutting, bidding and card-playing animations.
- Natural facial expressions and reactions.
- Animated chairs and body movement.
- Premium green-felt table with gold trim.
- Cut Throat Spades Masters branding on the table.
- Realistic card physics and placement.

No butler, servant, bartender, massage attendant or other NPC may occupy a player seat or become a fifth card player.

## 2. Interactive penthouse environment

The game room must be a living 3D environment.

Include:

- Panoramic city views.
- Luxury architecture and furnishings.
- Fireplace.
- Grand piano.
- Elegant lighting.
- Marble and polished surfaces.
- Leather seating.
- Bar and cigar lounge.
- Separate relaxation and massage area.
- Interactive doors, furniture and service locations.

Players must be able to interact with the environment, not merely look at a static background.

## 3. Interactive service characters

Create functional NPCs with realistic movement, animations, dialogue and interaction.

**Butler:**
- Responds when summoned.
- Approaches the requesting player.
- Takes service requests.
- Carries drinks and items on a tray.
- Serves and clears items.
- Returns to an appropriate position.

**Bartender:**
- Operates the bar.
- Prepares selected drinks.
- Performs serving animations.
- Responds to orders.

**Cigar attendant:**
- Operates the cigar lounge.
- Presents the cigar menu and accessories.
- Delivers purchased virtual items.
- Performs appropriate lounge interactions.

**Massage attendants:**
- Operate the relaxation area.
- Accept service bookings.
- Guide the player to the massage area.
- Perform appropriate massage animations.
- Complete the service and return to their assigned area.

These are service NPCs, not competitors.

## 4. Purchasable luxury services

Create an interactive service menu with:

- Butler service.
- Virtual drinks.
- Cigar selections and accessories.
- Massage bookings.
- Additional configurable luxury experiences.

Every purchase must:

- Display the price.
- Identify the payment currency.
- Require confirmation.
- Deduct the correct virtual currency.
- Record the purchase.
- Trigger the appropriate NPC interaction and animation.

Use virtual currency and optional premium currency. Services must not affect competitive card fairness.

## 6. Consistent gameplay across all destinations

All 23 destinations must support the same core game systems:

- Exactly four card players.
- The approved Cut Throat Spades Masters rules.
- Competitive AI.
- Realistic card animations.
- Interactive service NPCs.
- Purchasable services.
- Player profiles and rankings.
- Tournament and championship systems.

Each location must have its own atmosphere, soundtrack, ambient sounds, furniture, lighting and architectural identity.

## 7. Visual quality

The target is premium realistic 3D.

- No cartoon avatars.
- No generic flat game environments.
- No Victorian styling.
- No static human cutouts pretending to be 3D characters.
- No fifth player at the table.
- No identical rooms with different scenery.

Use animated 3D characters, realistic materials, lighting, shadows, reflections, appropriate camera movement and optimized assets.
