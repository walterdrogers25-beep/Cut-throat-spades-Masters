The House Rules

The Leagues

Walter's rules, exactly as he set them in the Masters brief. They override every older spec, every prototype, every house habit.

Solo League

Four players. Every soul for themselves.

  • Race to 250. You score for the books you bid, not the books you take — extra books past your bid score nothing.
  • Minimum bid 3. There is no nil in this house — nobody hides.
  • The Egg Rule: take zero books in a hand and your score resets to 0. All of it. Gone.
  • Bids of 3–6 pay 10 a book; bids of 7–13 pay 20 a book when they make — fortune favors the loud.
  • First hand, bid 7 or more and make it? The match ends on the spot. You take the room.
  • Miss your bid and you are set — ten points a bid book comes out of your score, with interest owed to the house's laughter.

Team League

Two against two. Trust is a currency — spend it carefully.

  • Race to 250. The combined bid is the law — you score for the books you bid.
  • Both partners must bid — bids from 0 to 13. Zero is allowed; there is no bonus for it. A team's combined bid is raised to 4 if it falls below 4.
  • Make the combined bid: 10 a book on bids 4–9, 20 a book on bids 10–13. Miss it: −10 a book. Together, either way.
  • No first-hand shortcut: a combined bid of 10 on the first hand scores like any other hand — it does not end the match.
  • A Boston — bid 11 or more and take all 13 books — ends the match the moment the last card falls. Bid 10 and take all 13? That is 200 points, not a Boston.
  • Throw-in: if both teams' combined bids add up to less than 11, the hand is thrown in and redealt — no score changes.
  • Two consecutive failed team hands and the match is over. No third chances.

— The House Code, Both Leagues —

  • The table stays in view at all times — you see every card, including your partner's plays.
  • Spades are trump, playable at any time — including leading the very first trick.
  • Restart, quit, and forfeit are on the table at any moment. No traps, no locked doors.
  • A renig must be caught and challenged to count — call it from the table and the house judges it. A confirmed renig costs the reneging side 4 books; a false call costs the accuser 4 books. The house AI never renigs on purpose.
  • No table talk in Team League: no chat, no voice, no hints. Enforced at the table.
  • Camera and mic at live tables are optional — and never touch who wins.
